What these samples are

These eight videos (four imaginary products, each in 10 and 30 seconds) are AI-generated. The 10-second clips were made in Google's AI video tool, Flow, from written descriptions. Each 30-second reel joins several AI-generated clips into one video. The mug, ring, candle and satchel are concepts: not real products and not for sale.

They were made from descriptions, not from a maker's photos, so they show the style and the kind of drift to expect, not the exact result for your product.

Your video starts from your own listing photos, so the product should look like yours. AI video can still change small details from one moment to the next, such as a stone's shape, a buckle or the number of objects. That is why a person checks every clip against your photos, and why we discard or re-make any clip that changes your product.

How the free test works

  1. You apply. Email us one listing link using the button below. It takes about two minutes.
  2. We write back. Within 3 days we email you. If we can help, we ask you to send the original photo files yourself (as attachments, or in a folder you share).
  3. We make a draft. We upload your photos to Google's Flow and make the clips by hand. A person watches every clip against your photos, and discards or re-makes any that change your product.
  4. You decide. We aim to send it within 72 hours of getting your photos. You look first. Ask for one round of changes, or post it, or do neither.

The video says only what you told us. We do not invent reviews, ratings, numbers, discounts or deadlines. Every video we show publicly is labelled as AI-generated, and we suggest you use the platform's own AI label when you post, if it asks for one.

Why it is free

We are documenting this project as a public case study of what small AI tools can and cannot do for small shops. You get a free video. As an option, you can let us show the finished video and describe the process. You can say no and still get your video.

We will never publish your sales numbers, your email or your photos without asking you first.

Your photos and your data

Only photos you send us are used. To make the video, we upload your photos to Google's Flow, which works under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We delete your photos, drafts and the Flow project 30 days after we deliver the video, or sooner if you ask. We keep the record of your request (your email, shop name, link and choices) for 12 months, then delete it.

Details are on the privacy page.

Questions

Do I have to pay anything?

No. There is no card, no account and no hidden step. This is a free test.

Will the video look like my product?

It starts from your photos, but AI video can change small details. A person checks every clip against your photos and discards or re-makes any that change your product. You look at the result too before anything is used. If you still see a mistake, tell us and we will fix it.

What do I need?

One listing, at least three clear, different photos of it, and the right to use them. Small or blurry photos can be declined, and we will tell you why.

Do you take photos from my shop?

No. We never copy photos from your shop page or from any website. You send the original files yourself.

Who can use the finished video?

You may post it on your own accounts. If you agree to the case-study option, we may also show it publicly with the AI label. You can withdraw that at any time.

Can you delete everything?

Yes. Email us and we will delete your photos, drafts and request record, and confirm by email.

Send us a listing

There is no web form on this page, and the page collects nothing. To ask for the free test, send us an email. The button opens your email program with a few questions already written in it: fill in the answers and send.
