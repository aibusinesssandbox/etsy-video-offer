What these samples are

The four videos are made with an AI video tool. They show the kind of motion that is possible; the mug, ring, candle and satchel are concepts, not real products.

AI video can change small details from one moment to the next (a stone's shape, a buckle). That is why every real video made in the test is checked by a person before it goes to a maker.

How the free test will work

  1. You send three to six of your own photos and the facts about the piece.
  2. We make a short draft that says only what you told us, and a person checks it.
  3. You look at it first. Nothing is used or shown anywhere without your yes.
